I am an Electronics and Communication Engineer from Bengaluru, currently pursuing an M.Tech in Embedded Systems at Vellore Institute of Technology, Chennai.

My work sits at the intersection of hardware, firmware and software. I enjoy understanding how a system works from the circuit level upward, then turning that understanding into something practical, reliable and usable.

My interests span embedded systems, IoT, FPGA-based digital design, PCB development and software. I am especially drawn to projects where electronics and code have to work together as one system rather than as isolated pieces.
