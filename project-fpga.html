PROJECT 01

MAR 2025 — JUL 2025

FPGA · VHDL · LOW-POWER DIGITAL DESIGN

32-BIT MULTIPLIER
& DIVIDER.

Design and implementation of a low-power 32-bit arithmetic processing unit on a Spartan-7-based Boolean FPGA board, with emphasis on power efficiency, resource utilization, timing and real-time hardware operation.

VHDL SPARTAN-7 VIVADO DSP48E1
01 — THE PROBLEM

Arithmetic is easy to describe.
Efficient hardware is not.

The project focused on implementing multiplication and division efficiently on a resource-constrained FPGA platform, balancing speed, power consumption and hardware resources.

02 — THE APPROACH

Four architectures.
One hardware decision.

01

DSP-BASED MULTIPLIER

Uses the FPGA's dedicated DSP48E1 resources with pipelining for high-speed arithmetic.

02

BOOTH MULTIPLIER

Reduces multiplication operations through Booth encoding of signed binary operands.

03

WALLACE TREE

Reduces partial products through layers of full and half adders.

04

NON-RESTORING DIVIDER

Uses iterative conditional addition/subtraction for sequential binary division.

a balancing act.

The reported results show the DSP-based multiplier achieving the lowest power among the compared multiplier architectures, while the non-restoring divider maintained low power without using DSP slices. Both designs met the 100 MHz timing constraint with no critical-path violations.

The designs were verified using Vivado simulation across 32-bit test vectors. Hardware implementation and timing analysis were then used to validate operation on the FPGA platform.
